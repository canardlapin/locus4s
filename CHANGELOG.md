# Changelog

## Unreleased

Compatible fixes and additions after 0.1.0 will be recorded here.

## 0.1.0

- Initial JVM and Scala.js release of `locus4s-core`, `locus4s-data`, and
  `locus4s-laws`.
- Established generative ownership, checked alignment, finite-domain algebras,
  neutral persistence, representation-neutral fields, deterministic
  aggregation, reusable laws, and executable documentation.
- Made CSR-limit failures explicit for relation identity, tabulation, CSR and
  row-based construction, converse, and reusable relation laws.
